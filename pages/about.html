Who we are

Our story

AgroNest is an agricultural enterprise based in Ndola, Copperbelt Province, Zambia. We bring crop production, livestock, practical farm services, training and organic manure together as parts of one connected farm.

Our work is shaped by the belief that strong local food systems depend on healthy soil, useful knowledge and lasting relationships among farmers, households, traders, institutions, restaurants and partners.

Our mission

To produce quality food and provide practical agricultural services and learning that support farmers, communities and a resilient local food system.

Our vision

A thriving agricultural future where sustainable production, shared knowledge and strong partnerships build better livelihoods.

Our values

The principles that guide how we farm, learn and work with others.

  • Quality

    We aim for care and consistency in our produce, farm practices and service to the community.

  • Sustainability

    We value responsible use of soil, water and other farm resources for future generations.

  • Community

    We believe agriculture grows stronger through local participation, learning and collaboration.

  • Integrity

    We strive to communicate honestly and build dependable relationships with everyone we serve.

  • Innovation

    We stay open to practical ideas and improved methods that can strengthen farm productivity.

From field to community

Our farming approach

We connect crop production—maize, soybeans, rape and tomatoes—with livestock production, including broilers, eggs and goats. Soil care, water management, farm learning and organic manure are part of our integrated approach.

Production decisions depend on season, site conditions and available resources. We encourage farmers to adapt recommendations to local conditions and seek qualified agricultural guidance when needed.

Our team

AgroNest brings together the people who care for farm production, share practical knowledge and build relationships with customers and partners. For team enquiries or collaboration, please get in touch.
